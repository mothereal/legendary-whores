## 2. Shared building blocks

**The five Arts** (always icon plus word): 🎀 **Silk** (finery, looks), 🪶 **Wit** (banter, verse, song), 🪙 **Gold** (money talk), 🎭 **Mask** (discretion, role-play), 🔥 **Frolic** (bawdy romps: strong, but they give you the Itch).

**The five Types**, one per Art: **Siren** "lives in her finery"; **Bluestocking** "seduces in iambic pentameter"; **Hustler** "knows the price of everything, including you"; **Enigma** "nobody knows her real name, possibly not even her"; **Minx** "has never once used the front door".

**Freshness:** Scrubbed ("clean as a new pin"), Fair ("passable"), Ripe ("hold your nose").

**Starting deck (10):** two Come-Hither Looks, two Saucy Quips, Teeth Extra, Peek-a-Boo Fan, A Saucy Wink (no Itch), Mother's Advice (no Art, so it never offends), plus her two signature cards. Each shared card has three or four era-specific flavour lines.

**Charms** (always on): Silver Tongue, Underestimated, Iron Constitution, Dimples, Good Listener, Old Flame, Knows Which Fork, Born in a Gin Shop.
**Talents** (once per Curtain): Double Entendre, Quick Change, Smokescreen, Read the Room, Upstage, Make Him Wait.
**Vices** (an upside and a downside): Loose Lips, Gambler, Bored Stiff, Vanity, Mother's Ruin, Jealousy.
