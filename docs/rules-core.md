# Legendary Whores · Prototype Rules Core (CANONICAL)

*The one rulebook the three prototypes share. It merges the two drafts: the onboarding, Best Guess and "nobody is ambushed" floor of `draft-accessible.md`, and the matchup ceiling (Tastes, Fancy, Kinks, History, House Rules) of `draft-deep.md`, restructured into **Timelines**. Source of truth above this file: the designer's private design spec. Where the drafts disagree with this file, this file wins.*

**Heat level: saucy postcard.** We wink; the door closes; a cherub covers its eyes. Never nipples, genitals, bare bottoms, depicted or graphic sex, or anyone not clearly an adult. Jokes target the job, the era and the punters, never anyone's ethnicity or religion.

**Scope freeze.** The slice has exactly three Timelines: **Victorian London**, **the Wild West** and **Modern Las Vegas**, all playable. Edo Yoshiwara and every other era are parked: no content and no art for them in this build. Nothing from the spec's parking lot is in here.

**The promise in one line:** play the cards that glow and you'll do fine; read the gentleman and you'll do splendidly.

## 0. The whole game on one phone screen

> You are a working girl in the **Eternal District**, where every era's red-light quarter sits on the same street. Each quarter is a **Timeline**.
> 1. **Pick a Place** for tonight. Each Place has a **host**, a gentleman with tastes.
> 2. **Play up to 3 cards** on him. Every card that suits him gets a green tick and scores more.
> 3. **Seal.** At the **Curtain** everyone at that Place is ranked. 1st takes the lion's share, 2nd and 3rd take smaller shares, and everyone who came gets a door gift.
> 4. Between Curtains, never sit still: take an **Assignation**, go **Rummaging** for coins and curious items, **Study** a gentleman, or hop to your whore in another Timeline.

### Three promises to the casual player (design rules, not tips)

1. **Hidden things only ever help.** Everything that can cost you (a gentleman's Aversion, a card he has seen before, your Itch, a door that will close) is printed face up. Secrets you uncover are only bonuses. Nobody loses to a trap she couldn't see.
2. **One button is always good enough.** **Best Guess** picks the cards that score highest on what you can see, and it never pushes your Itch to the point of catching something.
3. **Absence is not punished.** Miss a Curtain and your **Standing Order** plays Best Guess at your usual Place. Door gifts pay everyone who turns up.

### Plain words (each bold word has a tap-tooltip in the game)

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|

## 1. Timelines

The Eternal District is made of Timelines. Each is its own arena with its own **Curtain clock**, rivals, seats, three Places, three gentlemen, a market of 4 era cards, 4–5 novelties, 2 afflictions, 2 gag events, gossip and an era title ladder.

- **One whore per Timeline.** Your own whores never meet in the same Timeline, so you can never clash with yourself.
- **Cap: 3 active Timelines** per account (v1).
- **Unlocks.** You start with **one** whore: picking a starter picks her Timeline. The **second Timeline** unlocks the moment your first whore has finished her first Curtain **and** her first Assignation; it arrives as an invitation ("A telegram from the Wild West…") with a **Later** button, so nobody is forced to juggle. The **third** unlocks when any of your whores reaches **Rare**. In the slice the player reaches the second unlock.
- **Never idle without extra power.** When one whore is waiting for her Curtain, play another. Each whore has her own daily limits (§5, §6), so switching buys variety and insight, never a bigger hand.
- **Tourists.** Cross-Timeline visits exist only as comic solo **Tourist** gentlemen in Assignations (§8.5). Whores never travel.
- **Era skins.** All Timelines share one **house layer** (card anatomy, buttons, icons, layout, gestures). Each Timeline swaps only palette, textures, illustration treatment and display type (§13). Players never relearn controls.

## 3. Matchup math (exact numbers)

Everything is whole numbers. **No dice in any clash, ever.** The only unknown at a Curtain is what your rivals chose.

### 3.1 Card score

Each card you **Work** (play) scores:

```
card score = printed Allure
           + House Rule of the Place (e.g. Wit +1)   } this part never below 0
           + card text (e.g. "+1 if he lists Wit")    }
           + 1  ✓ it carries one of his Tastes          (once per card; visible)
           + 1  ✓ it carries his Secret Taste           (applies even if you don't know it)
           + 1  ✓ it carries your Signature Art         (visible)
           − 2  ✗ it carries his Aversion               (visible)
```

A card can earn up to three ✓ and one ✗ (a card that matches a Taste *and* the Aversion gets both). Unplayed cards are **pocketed**: you get their printed **Pocket** value in Coin (most are 0; Gold cards 1–2).

### 3.2 Sway (once per encounter, after the cards)

| Bonus | Amount | Visible? |
|---|---|---|
| **Fancy**: your Type is his Fancy | +2 | Yes |
| **Kink**: its trigger is met (an item, or a pattern of cards) | +3, once | No: Study 2, a Tell, or by accident (then it's in your Little Black Book) |
| **Regular**: earlier visits where you reached his Bar | +1 each, max +2 | Yes |
| **Grudge**: you fell short of his Bar last visit | −1, clears after your next success | Yes |
| **Seen It**: a card you Worked on him last visit | −1 per card (stamped on the card) | Yes |
| **Respectable** (Standing 5+) at a Posh Place | +1 | Yes |
| **Notorious** (Notoriety 5+) at a Rowdy or Gutter Place | +1 | Yes |
| **Grease Palms** (Notoriety 3+) at a Rowdy or Gutter Place | +1 per 2 Coin, max +2 per Curtain (+3 at Notoriety 5, +4 at Notoriety 8; round 4) | Yes |
| **Brave Face** | +1 | Yes |
| Charm, Talent, Vice, novelty item (max 1 item per encounter) | as printed | Yes |

**Sway never goes below 0.**

### 3.3 The Itch (the push-your-luck)

Frolic cards are printed about 1 Allure stronger than other cards of the same price. The cost is the **Itch**:

| His Freshness | Itch from Worked Frolic cards |
|---|---|
| **Scrubbed** | none |
| **Fair** | +1 per Frolic card |
| **Ripe** | +1 per Frolic card, and +1 more for the encounter |

- **A Saucy Wink** gives no Itch ("a wink never gave anyone anything"). **The Wheelbarrow** counts as two Frolic cards.
- **Protection** subtracts from each encounter's Itch: Iron Constitution 1, the Lambskin Sheath 2.
- **At Itch 3 you catch** the Affliction carried by the gentleman who pushed you over (a Scrubbed gentleman carries nothing). It goes into your discard pile, Itch resets to 0, Notoriety +1, and a 2-second gag plays.
- **Itch fades:** −1 after each Curtain in which you gained none.
- The Sway meter always shows "Itch 2 → 3: you'll catch the Saddle Sores. Fancy it?" before you seal. **Best Guess never takes you to 3.** Catching something is always a choice.

### 3.4 Why matchups matter

The same card swings by up to 6 points depending on whom it's aimed at. The Limerick from Nantucket (Wit + Frolic, Allure 3) played by Dolly at the Salon on Lord Plunkett: 3 + 1 (Wit house) − 1 (Frolic house) + 1 (Taste Wit) + 1 (Signature Wit) − 2 (Aversion Frolic) = **3**, plus 1 Notoriety for Frolic at a Posh Place. The same card by Dolly at the Tuppenny Palace on Alfie Barrow (loves Frolic): 3 + 1 (Taste Frolic) + 1 (Signature) = **5**, and +1 Itch because Alfie is Fair. The right Place for Dolly is not the right Place for Jackie, and the right three cards for Lord Plunkett are wrong for Alfie.

## 4. The evening and the Curtain

An **evening** is the stretch between two Curtains in one Timeline.

### 4.1 The loop

| Step | What happens | Casual taps |
|---|---|---|
| **Dawn deal** | Draw 5 cards (your Curtain hand). Tonight's rota lights up: which gentleman hosts which Place, and the rota for the next 3 Curtains. | 0 |
| **Between Curtains** (any order, as often as you like) | Assignations (§5), Study and Rummaging (§6), shopping, inspecting (long-press flips any card, gentleman, rival or item), switching Timelines. | optional |
| **Plan** | Tap a Place (smileys 0–3 show how well it suits you on visible info). Drag up to 3 cards onto the host, or tap **Best Guess**. Optionally add 1 novelty and your Talent. The live Sway meter shows your exact Sway from what you know; unknowns show as "?". | 2–3 |
| **Seal** | Press the wax seal. You may unseal and change your mind until the Curtain falls. | 1 |
| **Curtain** | Rooms resolve, shares pay out, the curtain-drop animation plays. | 0 |
| **Dawn housekeeping** | Itch check and fade, History updates, played and pocketed cards go to your discard pile (reshuffled into a new draw pile when it runs out). | 0 |

A casual evening is **three taps**: Place, Best Guess, seal.

**Smileys, exactly.** For each open Place, take the Best Guess play's *visible* Sway minus the Place's Bar: **3 smileys** at +4 or more, **2** at +1 to +3, **1** at −1 to 0, **none** below −1. The Standing Order and the casual bot pick the most smileys, breaking ties by the bigger 1st-place Renown.

**Best Guess, exactly.** It tries every set of 1–3 cards in your hand and picks the highest *visible* Sway, with three guards: it never takes your Itch to 3; in the Society Pages it counts each point of Notoriety a play would cost as −1 Sway (Pick His Pocket as −2), so it never drifts you out of that paper by accident; and a Curtain play that ends below the Bar never buys Itch (any Itch it adds ranks it last). It uses no items and no Talent.

**Which paper she's in (round 7).** Nobody picks a road. Each whore is in the **Society Pages** (Standing) or the **Police Gazette** (Notoriety), and her meters decide which: the meter that leads puts her in its paper (a 1-point lead is enough: `RULES.paperLead` 1, the designer's call), and while the two are level she stays in the paper she was in (§9). Her paper is settled **once per action** (a Curtain, an Assignation, a cure, a clock advance), on the net move, so a flip and a flip back inside one Curtain is no news. Each action gives a whore at most one private `paper` event: she changed papers, or a warning that one more point the other way would change them (`roadTurn(w).steps` has just dropped to 1). Best Guess's guard, the smileys, the casual Place pick, the Standing Order, her title, the digest's TONIGHT pick and the Ladder all follow her paper. A whore already in the Police Gazette is not capped at 1 smiley on her first Gutter visit and is not asked "Go slumming?". Engine: `roadOf`, `roadTurn`, `view.whore.road`, `view.whore.roadTurn`, `w.paper`. A save from before round 7 keeps a declared road only as a tie-breaker, dropped at her next meter move. *(Round 4 to 6: a declared, reversible road, `setRoad`. Round 7 removed it.)*

### 4.2 The Curtain clock (per Timeline)

- The Curtain falls when **every active whore in that Timeline has sealed** (Automatons seal at once; "active" = played in the last 24 hours), or **3 hours** after the previous Curtain, whichever comes first, and **never sooner than 20 minutes** after it.
- **Standing Order:** an unsealed whore plays Best Guess at the open Place with the **most smileys** tonight (§4.1; ties to the bigger 1st-place Renown; a first Gutter visit is declined, as for the casual bot). She is paid normally. A UI shows this pick before the Curtain falls (`standingOrderPick`), and the prototype's District clock waits while any of your whores is at last call.
- **Display (the Scandal Sheet):** the District clock runs one District minute per real second of play, and only while she is on a play screen with nothing open. The Scandal Sheet uses `minGapMin` 15 (`game/slice-config.js`) against the rulebook's 20. The page never shows the clock in hours and minutes. It shows words (`curtainWhen`: later on, soon, any minute now, last call), and they change at most once a minute. Display only: no rule reads them.
- **Full pay for 3 Curtains a day.** Each whore's first **3** Curtains per district day (the day turns at 06:00 district time) pay Renown. Later Curtains that day are **After Hours**: Coin, door gifts and History only; an After Hours Curtain moves **neither meter** (no Standing for a Posh placing, no Notoriety from the Gutter or Frolic) and builds **no Itch**, so letting time pass can't farm a route *(B-arcade round 2, finding 3)*. This is the main "time can't buy rank" guard, and the reason to go and play another Timeline. **To bed:** when all your whores are After Hours, the prototype offers *sleep till dawn* (`sleepTillDawn`): the clock jumps to 06:00 of the next district day, every Timeline's Curtain clock restarts, nothing resolves overnight.

### 4.3 Places and the split (Places are the same shape in every Timeline)

| Kind | Door | Bar | Renown 1st / 2nd / 3rd | Coin 1st / 2nd / 3rd | Applause (1st, if 2+ whores came) | Door gift | Meter effect |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Posh** | Standing 2+ **and** Standing ≥ Notoriety | 10 | 9 / 4 / 2 | 0 / 0 / 0 | +1 Renown | 1 Coin | 1st or 2nd: Standing +1. Each Frolic card Worked here: Notoriety +1 (unless Sign the NDA; never at the Velvet Rope Penthouse, whose House Rule includes an NDA). |
| **Rowdy** | always open | 7 | 6 / 3 / 1 | 1 / 0 / 0 | +1 Renown (Tuppenny Palace +2) | 1 Coin | none |
| **Gutter** | always open (first visit asks "Go slumming?") | 6 | 8 / 3 / 1 | 3 / 2 / 1 | none | 1 Coin | Attending: Notoriety +1 (unless Sign the NDA) |

- **Only whores who reach the Bar take a share**, ranked by Sway. Everyone who came gets the door gift.
- **Below the Bar:** door gift and a **Brave Face** (+1 Sway next Curtain), plus a Grudge with that host.
- **Ties (dead heat):** tied whores share a rank and **split the combined shares of the places they occupy, rounded up** (Applause too). Two tied for 1st at a Posh Place each take ceil((9 + 4) / 2) = 7 Renown + 1 Applause, against 10 for an outright winner. No coin flips, but one more point of Sway still pays. *(Changed 2026-10-07, B-arcade review finding 8: "all take the higher share" made drawing level worth exactly as much as winning.)*
- **4th and lower** (above the Bar): door gift only. Unclaimed shares stay with the house.
- **History:** reaching the host's Bar makes it a Regular visit (+1, max +2).

**Worked split.** The Tuppenny Palace (Rowdy, Bar 7), host Alfie Barrow: Dolly 15, a second whore 12, a third 9, a fourth 6. Dolly: 6 + Applause 2 = **8 Renown**, 1 + 1 Coin. 2nd: **3 Renown**, 1 Coin. 3rd: **1 Renown**, 1 Coin. 4th (below Bar): 1 Coin and a Brave Face. Pot paid: 12 Renown, two thirds of it to 1st.

### 4.4 The rota and Raid Night

- **Rota.** Each Timeline's three gentlemen rotate through its three Places, one step per Curtain (host A at Posh, B at Rowdy, C at Gutter; next Curtain C, A, B; and so on). The rota shows 3 Curtains ahead, so "which gentleman, at which Place, under which House Rule" is the plan you make. Yes, the cracksman sometimes hosts the Salon. He came in through the window.
- **Raid Night.** Every 3rd Curtain in a Timeline the Gutter Place is raided (the Peelers / the Marshal / Metro Vice): its **Renown shares are halved, rounded down**; Coin is unaffected. Shown on the rota 3 Curtains ahead. (Added because, without it, Notoriety players never left the Gutter.)

## 5. Assignations (instant solo jobs)

A quick one-on-one with a computer-run gentleman, resolved the instant you tap. No waiting, no rivals.

1. The **Assignation board** shows your Timeline's gentlemen: the Scrubbed and Fair ones always; the Ripe one (a **back-alley** Assignation) once your Notoriety is 1+; and a **Tourist** when one is passing through (§8.5).
2. Your deck **lends you 3 random cards** (your Curtain hand is untouched; Afflictions can turn up and take a slot).
3. Work **1 or 2** of them; add up to 1 novelty; your Talent if unused this Curtain. Same matchup math, no House Rule.
4. **His Bar:** Scrubbed 6, Fair 5, Ripe 4.

| Result | Assignations 1–3 today (per whore) | 4–6 | 7+ |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Satisfied** (Sway ≥ Bar) | 2 Renown + 1 Coin (back-alley: 3 Coin + 1 Coin per Frolic card Worked, Notoriety +1) | 1 Renown + 1 Coin | a Gossip, no Coin (no gentleman's Coin hook either) *(B-arcade round 2, finding 4: the Richest board must not rank how often you tap)* |
| **Delighted** (Sway ≥ Bar + 3) | as Satisfied, +1 Renown, +1 Gossip; Scrubbed gentleman: Standing +1 | as Satisfied, +1 Gossip | as above |
| **Fizzled** (below Bar) | nothing; Grudge −1 next time; his Aversion is stamped in your Black Book if you hit it | same | same |

- Itch applies (§3.3). A Kink hit is free here: no Notoriety ("it's private").
- **Ceiling: 9 Renown a day per whore from Assignations**, against about 15–23 from three full-pay Curtains.
- Every Assignation still teaches (Aversions, Kinks and Secret Tastes you hit go in your Black Book) and builds History, but a gentleman's Regular count rises at most once per day.

## 6. Study and Rummaging (time buys insight and options, not power)

### 6.1 Study ("Watch from the bar")
A 3-second vignette of the gentleman being himself. Each Study reveals the next fact, permanently for the season:

1. **Secret Taste** (now shows as a ✓ source on your cards)
2. **Kink** (the item or card pattern that triggers +3)
3. **History** (which rival last charmed him, and with what cards)

**A whisper decodes a Tell.** A Kink novelty offered behind a back door comes with a stallholder's whisper that quotes one gentleman's public Tell. **Buying it decodes that Tell: you learn his Kink** (that fact only; it goes in your Little Black Book). *(2026-10-07, B-arcade finding 9.)*

**Gossip looks ahead.** Trading 1 Gossip on a rival's profile tells you where she was last Curtain **and where she is heading this Curtain** (a human's sealed Place; an Automaton's or stand-in's Habit pick as it stands; "following you about" while the prototype script runs). *(2026-10-07, B-arcade finding 3.)*

Studying a **rival** reveals, in order, her **Habit**, her **Vice**, and her last 3 Places. **3 Studies per whore per district day are free; after that 1 Coin each** ("bribing the maid"). Each Timeline holds only 9 gentleman facts and 6 rival facts, so a heavy player soon knows everything there is, and a light player who reads Tells (free) knows most of what matters.

### 6.2 Rummaging (Exploration)
Tap a Place's back door. Each Rummage is seeded and instant:

| Rummage # today (per whore) | Finds |
|---|---|
| 1st–3rd ("fresh stalls") | 1 in 3: 2 Coin · 1 in 3: a novelty offered for sale (buy or pass; black-market items only at Notoriety 2+) · 1 in 6: a Gossip (a Tell decoded, or a rival's leaning) · 1 in 6: a collectible saucy postcard |
| 4th onward ("picked over") | 1 in 2: 1 Coin · 1 in 2: Gossip or a postcard |

**Market:** every stall in a Timeline sells that Timeline's 4 era cards (§10.4) at any time; bought cards go into your discard pile. **Cures** are sold at the stall named in §10.6.

### 6.3 What a heavy player gains (and doesn't)

| | Light player (two visits a day) | Heavy player (on all day) |
|---|---|---|
| Curtains | Same 3 full-pay Curtains a day; Standing Orders cover the rest | Same |
| Renown from Assignations | ~2–4 a day | Capped at 9 a day |
| Hidden facts known | Most within 3–4 days (Tells, accidents, free Studies) | All by day 2 |
| Items | 1–2 in the Reticule | Always the right 3 |
| Net edge | | Insight and options; never a bigger hand, extra plays per Curtain or stronger numbers per card |

The **Reticule** (handbag) holds 3 novelties; 1 per encounter.

## 7. Charms, Talents and Vices

Every whore starts with one of each; more come from tiers, side jobs and the Hall (not in the slice). **Charm:** always on. **Talent:** once per Curtain cycle, at the Curtain or at one Assignation. **Vice:** a flaw with a laugh and an upside.

| Charm (8) | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Silver Tongue** | Your first Wit card each encounter counts as matching his Taste even if he doesn't list Wit. |
| **Underestimated** | +2 Sway in a clash where you have the least Renown at your Place. |
| **Iron Constitution** | Protection 1: blocks 1 Itch per encounter. |
| **Dimples** | +1 Sway against anyone whose Tastes include Silk. |
| **Good Listener** | Each Study reveals 2 facts. |
| **Old Flame** | Start each season as a Regular (1 visit) with one gentleman of your choice in your Timeline. |
| **Knows Which Fork** | +2 Sway at Posh Places. |
| **Born in a Gin Shop** | +2 Sway at Gutter Places; Grease Palms costs 1 Coin per +1 instead of 2. |

| Talent (6) | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Double Entendre** | One Worked card gains an Art of your choice for this encounter. |
| **Quick Change** | Swap one card in your Curtain hand for the top card of your deck. |
| **Smokescreen** | Your Frolic cards give no Itch this encounter; Notoriety +1 (you were seen leaving by the window). |
| **Read the Room** | While planning, reveal one hidden fact about tonight's host. |
| **Upstage** | In a clash, the rival placed directly above you loses 2 Sway. |

## 9. Standing vs Notoriety (the two routes)

Two opposed meters per whore, 0–10, both public on her portrait (Standing as a pearl necklace that lengthens, Notoriety as a blush that deepens).

- **Seesaw:** each time one meter rises by 1, the other falls by 1 (never below 0).
- **Start:** Standing 2, Notoriety 0.
- **Which paper she's in** *(round 7, §4.1)*: nobody picks it; the meters do.

  | Meters | Her paper |
  |---|---|
  | Standing > Notoriety | the Society Pages (`'standing'`) |
  | Notoriety > Standing | the Police Gazette (`'notoriety'`) |
  | level | the paper she was in (`w.paper`) |

  From the start (2/0), one Gutter night takes her to 1/1: still the Society Pages, and the warning. A second takes her to 0/2: the Police Gazette. Back again takes two Standing gains (1/1 holds the Gazette, 2/0 is the Society Pages). When the gap is odd (a meter stuck at 0, a Notoriety −1 card, a 3/2 night), one point the other way changes her paper, so the warning comes one point ahead, not always a night ahead. Settled once per action, with at most one `paper` event (§4.1). The doors stay on the raw meters (Posh: Standing 2+ and Standing ≥ Notoriety); the paper steers advice, labels, titles and the Ladder.
- **Posh doors** are open while Standing is 2+ **and** Standing ≥ Notoriety. **Gutter doors** are always open; your first visit asks "Go slumming? Notoriety +1, Standing −1." **Back-alley** Assignations appear at Notoriety 1+. **Black-market** novelties appear at Notoriety 2+.
- **Respectable** (Standing 5+): +1 Sway at Posh Places; may challenge for the **Salon Seat**.
- **Notorious** (Notoriety 5+): +1 Sway at Rowdy and Gutter Places; may challenge for the **Gutter Throne**.
- **Grease Palms** (Notoriety 3+): at a Rowdy or Gutter Place, pay Coin per +1 Sway (2 at Common, 3 at Rare, 4 at Epic, 5 above: `greasePer`, round 5), max +2 per Curtain; +3 at Notoriety 5 and +4 at Notoriety 8 (round 4, `greaseMax`).
- **The Society Pages road's own rungs** *(round 5, mirroring the Police Gazette's back alleys, black market and Front Page)*, each while Standing ≥ Notoriety:
  - **Invitations** (Standing 3+): Scrubbed gentlemen invite her. The first invitation she **Delights** each day adds **+1 Renown on top of the daily Assignation cap**; Coin as usual (`RULES.highRoad.invitationRenown`).
  - **A Patron** (Standing 7+): 2 Coin in an unsigned envelope at each new day (paid to any whore who qualifies, stand-ins included).
  - **The Society Pages, framed** (Standing 10, once a season): a collectible, the Front Page's twin.
- **Renown milestones** *(round 5)* at **100** and **200** Renown, between Rare (30) and Epic (350): an era sub-title worn under her title (`ERA_MILESTONES`, invented, in each era's voice), announced once a season.
- **The Ladder** *(round 5)*: four rungs of lodgings and finery per road and Timeline (`DIGS`, e.g. Victorian Society Pages: a gown from Worth, a carriage and pair, a box at Covent Garden, a villa in St John's Wood; Police Gazette: a room over the pie shop, a parlour with a piano, the Forty Elephants' protection, a house of her own in Wapping), bought in order with Coin on the road she is on and kept if she changes road. Cosmetic: it outlines her portrait, labels her winning picture and lists on her stats; it never adds Sway (T9).
- **The Morning Special** *(round 5)*: at each dawn one novelty from the Timeline's stalls is on the counter, chosen by a hash of the seed, Timeline and day (no RNG draw); one per whore per day; black-market items still need Notoriety 2+; buying a Kink novelty decodes its Tell, as at the stall.
- **Squaring the Peelers** (a lever, off): a Notorious whore may pay Coin on Raid Night to keep her full Gutter Renown (`RULES.raidBribe`, `plan.bribe`). Built and tested, but off: measured in round 4, it pulls the Notoriety planner back into the Gutter on Raid Nights and fails T3 and T4 (Balance log, round 4).
- **Notoriety 8+:** Scrubbed gentlemen refuse your Assignations ("His Lordship is not at home"). The way back from there is each era's Notoriety −1 market card: The Charity Bazaar (London), The Temperance Pledge (Dakota), The Chapel Quickie (Vegas). Her stats and a shut Posh door point to it *(8 Oct 2026, the designer's decision: a card, not a rule change)*.
- **Front Page** (Notoriety 10, once a season): the gossip sheet runs your portrait; you get a framed front page collectible and a gold rule round your row on the Most Notorious board. No penalty: you've earned it.

### 9.1 Seats (teaser; not played in the slice)

Per Timeline: **2 Legendary seats** (the **Salon Seat**, Standing 5+; the **Gutter Throne**, Notoriety 5+) and **1 Mythic seat** (the **Crown**: you must hold a Legendary seat and have Standing or Notoriety 7+). An Epic whore who meets the meter may **Challenge** a holder: a Duel at the next Curtain; the challenger picks the Place, the holder picks the judge from that Timeline's three gentlemen; the holder gets +1 Sway (home crowd). Lose and the holder drops to Epic. A seat can be challenged once per Curtain; a holder can't be challenged for 2 Curtains after a defence; a failed challenger waits 2 days for that seat. Vacant seats go to the first eligible whore to win a Duel night at that seat's Place. Holders at season's end hang in the **Hall of Legendary Whores** (Gutter Throne holders in its **Rogues' Gallery** wing). Seats and Halls stay per Timeline.

## 11. Whorescore and the boards

### 11.1 Whorescore (account level)

Each season, each of your whores scores **tier points for the best result she held that season** in her Timeline:

| Best result this season | Common | Rare | Epic | **Legendary seat** | **Mythic seat** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Tier points | **1** | **4** | **13** | **40** | **121** |

- **Rule of thumb: each rung is worth three of the rung below, plus one.** So one mastered Timeline always beats three skimmed ones at the rung below: 1 Epic (13) > 3 Rares (12); 1 Legendary seat (40) > 3 Epics (39); 1 Mythic seat (121) > 3 Legendary seats (120).
- A whore scores only once she **has a result**: a Curtain played or Renown earned. Opening a Timeline is not a result, so it is worth 0 until she plays there. *(2026-10-07, B-arcade finding 17.)*
- A seat counts if held through at least one 06:00 Dawn.
- **Best 3 whores per season count in full; any others count half** (rounded down). With the v1 cap of 3 active Timelines this only bites if you retire a whore and start a new one in the same season.
- Whorescore is **cumulative across seasons**; ties are broken by total season Renown.
- *Why not the designer's example numbers (1 / 3 / 8 / 25 / 60)?* With them, three Rares (9) beat one Epic (8), so a planner who reaches Epic in one Timeline but misses a seat loses to a casual player who skims three Timelines to Rare. The ×3+1 ladder keeps the designer's shape and makes "depth beats breadth" hold at every rung. To be confirmed by the season simulation (§14).

### 11.2 Players screen

One screen, four tabs: **Whorescore** · **Richest** · **Most Notorious** · **Most Respectable**.

| Board | Ranks players by |
|---|---|
| Whorescore | Lifetime Whorescore |
| Richest | The most Coin **one** of your whores earned this season (ties: your second whore's) *(round 5)* |
| Most Notorious | Your **best single whore's** season-peak Notoriety (ties: your second whore's, then total Renown) *(round 5)* |
| Most Respectable | Your **best single whore's** season-peak Standing (ties: your second whore's, then total Renown) *(round 5)* |

*Round 5 (depth beats breadth):* these boards used to sum all your whores, so three whores at 4 beat one at 10 and the road boards rewarded breadth. They now rank the best single whore.

Each row: rank, username, the board's number, and a chip per active whore (portrait, Timeline badge, era title, e.g. "Dolly Mopp · London · dress lodger"). Tap a row for the **public profile**: each whore's portrait and era title, tier, public Standing and Notoriety, last 3 Curtain results, Charm (public), Talent and Vice (shown only once you've Studied her), collectible shelf, Hall plaques. **Automatons** appear in a separate "House Automatons" section, always with the 🗝 badge, never ranked on Whorescore.

## 12. While You Were Away (the digest)

Shown on login (across all your Timelines) and when you switch into a Timeline (that Timeline only). A gossip sheet, never a notification flood: **at most 5 headlines, at least 1**. Tap a headline for the detail card. No push notifications in v1, except an opt-in one when your seat is challenged.

### 12.1 Ranking (the engine must do this)

Every world event is logged with a type, the whores and gentlemen it involves, and the Curtain number. When the digest is built for a player, each unseen event gets:

```
relevance = base weight (table)
          × 1.5 if it names your whore or a gentleman you're a Regular with
          × 1.3 if it involves the rival who last beat you
          × 0.85 ^ (Curtains since the event)
```

| Event type | Base |
|---|---|
| Your seat challenged, won or lost; your challenge repelled; an empty seat you bid for missed | 100 |
| Your own challenge or empty-seat bid, still pending (a reminder; dropped once the Duel is decided) | 45 |
| Your whore promoted; a Timeline unlocked | 90 |
| Your Standing Order results (collapsed into **one** headline per Timeline) | 70 |
| A rival overtook you on the Timeline's Renown table or on a board | 60 |
| A rival Delighted a gentleman you're a Regular with | 50 |
| Rota: in the next 3 Curtains, a gentleman whose Fancy is your Type hosts a Place you can enter | 45 |
| A stall now sells a Kink item for a gentleman whose Kink you know | 45 |
| Raid Night on a Gutter Place you visited in your last 3 Curtains | 40 |
| A rival you've Studied changed her Habit | 35 |
| Someone caught an Affliction from a gentleman you've Frolicked with | 30 |
| District gossip (others' gag events, Front Pages) | 15 |

- **Show:** every event scoring **25 or more**, best first, capped at **5**. If fewer than 3 qualify, top up to 3 with the best gossip. If nothing happened at all: one line, *"Nothing stirred. Even the cat was bored."*
- **Collapse duplicates** of the same type about the same subject into one headline ("Three Curtains by Standing Order: 2nd, 1st, door gift. +11 Renown.").
- **What she learned while you were out:** a Curtain she did not see (her sealed plan fell while she was elsewhere, or her Standing Order went out) also says what she learned by accident there, as the end of that Curtain's line; it takes no headline of its own, and while it carries that news the line does not age (the fact is on her cards now).
- **Voice:** gossip-sheet headlines, one line, classy and rude. Examples: *"SEAT UNDER SIEGE: Lady Lavinia demands your chair. Pistols at the next Curtain."* · *"PIANO LEGS IN PERIL: Lord P. to host the Tuppenny Palace on Thursday. He likes a Bluestocking."* · *"STOP PRESS: Brass Bettie pips you on the Richest board by a single, suspicious coin."*

### What thinking buys (the levers, for the in-game help card)

1. **Pick the Place for your whore and tonight's host**, not the biggest pot. Read the rota 3 Curtains ahead.
2. **Read Tells, then Study**, to light up Secret Tastes and Kinks.
3. **Bring the right item.** 2–3 Coin for +3 is the best trade on the street, but only on the right man.
4. **Pick your route.** Standing for Renown and Applause, Notoriety for Coin, bribes and the Gutter Throne; don't drift.
5. **Predict the rivals.** Automaton Habits are public once Studied; dodge them or crowd them.
6. **Rotate cards** to avoid Seen It, and save Regulars for the contested nights.
7. **Ride the Itch.** Two Frolic cards tonight on a Fair man, a clean night tomorrow, and you never see the quack.

## 17. The slice, in order

1. Sign-up and the first 60 seconds (§16), in the chosen starter's Timeline and skin.
2. Evening 1: pick a Place; Best Guess or hand-pick; seal.
3. Curtain with a clash and a **split reward**. The slice guarantees the clash at a Timeline's **first Curtain only** (`scriptRival: { curtains: 1 }`): the Timeline's rival goes where the player goes (labelled "scripted for the prototype"); after that her Habit drives her, so "predict the rivals: dodge them or crowd them" works. The other seats are filled by labelled filler whores. In London the first Curtain is staged as §15 (`stageRivals`: Lady Lavinia holds Swan Neck, Strict Governess and Come-Hither Look, is a Regular +2 of Lord Plunkett and keeps her Talent for later), so Best Guess places 2nd and the thinking play wins: in The Arcade, Best Guess 14 (2nd, +4) against Study + the Cane 17 (1st, +10), 19 with Double Entendre.
4. One **Assignation** (any gentleman on the board).
5. An **inspect/flip** moment: a gentleman's card (Tells, History, "?" chips; tap a "?" to Study) and a novelty item.
6. The **second Timeline unlocks** (telegram); the player may switch, sees that Timeline's skin and a **While You Were Away** digest of 3 seeded headlines.
7. The **Players screen** with the four boards and a rival's public profile (Automatons labelled).

## 18. Judging notes (why the merge looks like this)

| Criterion | Accessible draft | Deep draft | Canonical choice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Fun** | Delightful first minute, gag events, three-tap evenings; matchups a bit flat (one Taste, one Kink) | Rich puzzles (Fancy, Kinks with card patterns, House Rules, History), positions as cards | Accessible's first minute and gags; Deep's matchup toys |
| **Newcomer clarity** | Excellent: three promises, Best Guess, Standing Orders, all penalties face up | Weaker: hidden Aversions and Freshness can ambush; Work/Purse and Openers add early decisions | Accessible's promises are law: Aversion and Freshness public; Openers and the Work/Purse split cut (pocketing kept, simplified to printed Pocket values) |
| **Matchup depth** | Ticks + Charm-vs-gentleman Type; Kink +3; little reason to change Place | Two Tastes, Aversion, Fancy, Kink, History, House Rules, per-target Afflictions | Deep's model, plus the rota (hosts move between Places), two routes and per-Timeline House Rules |
| **Buildability** | Simple state; Itch meter is one integer | More state (Opener, Tips economy, Risk vs Freshness) | One currency (Coin), one Itch integer, Freshness as 3 public levels; everything whole numbers, no dice |
| **Evidence** | Estimate only (1.4×) | Rough sim (1.36–1.47×) with an all-knowing planner | New designer sim on the merged rules: 1.38–1.52×; honest fails listed in §14.2 |

Calls I made that the designer may want to overrule:
- **Whorescore ladder 1 / 4 / 13 / 40 / 121** instead of the example 1 / 3 / 8 / 25 / 60 (§11.1).
- **Seats are per Timeline** (2 Legendary + 1 Mythic each, so 6 + 3 in a 3-Timeline world), per the spec's "Seats and Halls stay per Timeline"; that is more than v1's "about 5 Legendary and 1–2 Mythic per world".
- **The second Timeline unlocks on day one** (after one Curtain and one Assignation) so the slice can show it; it comes as an optional invitation, which keeps the spec's "no juggling on day one" in spirit.
- **Freshness is public and the Itch is deterministic**, so catching something is always a visible choice. The cost: in the sim, almost nobody catches anything (T8 fails). If playtests agree, pay Ripe gentlemen more for Frolic.
- **Fondness (Accessible) is replaced by History** (Deep's Regular / Grudge / Seen It), which does the same job with more texture.
- Known open fails to fix by tuning before the designer's pick: **T3 and T4 in Vegas, T8 everywhere; T5 and T9 unsimulated.** *Update (engine sim, §14.3): after tuning only **T4 in Vegas** still fails (Motel Paradiso 46%); it needs a rule-level choice, not a number.* *Update 2026-10-07: T4 is closed (Candy Floss works the Motel, the Penthouse NDA, the Velvet Spur without Gold −1); all eleven targets pass. See "T4 closed" in the §14.3 Balance log.*
