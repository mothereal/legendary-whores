# Victorian London art: file map for builders

Generated by `art-src/victorian/generate.py files` from `manifest.json`; do not hand-edit. Every name below is the filename `engine/content.js` / `engine/ASSETS.md` asks for. **Painted** files exist under that name. **Stand-in** names have no file of their own: load the file in the *Load* column (same folder) instead, and crop with `object-fit: cover` (portraits: `object-position: top`).

Skin: gilded oil painting (Titian glow, Fragonard comedy, Hogarth satire), candlelit, gaslight and fog, gold frames; palette oxblood, gilt, soot, parchment. Model: FLUX.2-klein-4B (4-bit, local). Every image was opened and reviewed by eye before acceptance; rejected attempts and reasons are in `manifest.json`.

Sizes: portraits 675x900 (3:4), places 1200x675 (16:9), items/afflictions/cards 600x600, postcards 900x600 (3:2), `skin-bg` 1024x1024 seamless tile, `skin-frame` and `skin-card` 768x1024 (9-slice), `skin-curtain` 1280x1600, `skin-divider` 1200x300 with transparent background.

Budget (prototype brief, 2026-10-07): about 25-35 painted images for this era, one per subject, expression variants only for the starter (Dolly: pleased, caught, wink; her blush reuses the resting plate). Lady Lavinia's four expression plates were accepted before that budget and are kept: finished work is not redone or thrown away.
