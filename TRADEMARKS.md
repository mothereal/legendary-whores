The code, art and writing in this repository are licensed under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE).
That licence covers copyright and patents. **It does not cover the name.** Section 6 of the licence
says so: it grants no permission to use the licensor's trade names, trademarks or product names.

## What is reserved

- The name **Legendary Whores**, the **Scandal Sheet** masthead and the game's logo and wordmarks.
- The domain **legendarywhores.com** and anything presented as the official game.

## What you're welcome to do

- **Fork it, change it, run it, sell it**: the Apache licence lets you. Please give your version
  its own name and look, so nobody mistakes it for the original.
- **Say what it's based on.** "Based on Legendary Whores" or "a fork of Legendary Whores" in your
  description or credits is fine and appreciated.
- **Fan art, streams, videos, reviews, mods and guides** that use the name to talk about the
  game: go ahead. No permission needed.

## What needs permission

- Publishing a game, app, site or merchandise **called** Legendary Whores, or using the logo or
  masthead as your own branding.
- Implying that a fork, server or product is official or endorsed.

Ask through the repository's issues. Being asked is welcome; being impersonated isn't.
